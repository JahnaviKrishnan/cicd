Course Selection Roadmap

Follow this roadmap to complete your course selection process.

Step 1: Semester 1 Course Selection

  • Select your batch (1-4, 5, or 6-11)
  • View available courses for your batch
  • Select courses up to a maximum of 45 credits
  • Proceed to Semester 2 selection

Step 2: Semester 2 Course Selection

  • Search for courses you've already completed in Semester 2
  • Select all relevant courses
  • Ensure you don't exceed 45 credits for the semester
  • Proceed to Semester 3 selection

Step 3: Semester 3 Course Selection

  • Select your specialization
  • View recommended courses based on your specialization and core requirements
  • Select courses up to a maximum of 45 credits
  • Proceed to the summary page

Step 4: Review and Submit

  • Review your course selections for all three semesters
  • Verify credit counts for each semester
  • Print your course plan for your records
  • Make changes if needed by going back to previous steps
